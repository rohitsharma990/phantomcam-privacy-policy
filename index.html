1. Overview

PhantomCam is a browser extension designed to provide camera effects, including switching between a saved room background and a live camera view. Depending on the features you use, it may access camera input, microphone input for clap detection, a room image you select, and extension settings.

2. Permissions and access

  • Camera: Used for camera-related features when enabled and permitted by you.
  • Microphone: If clap detection is enabled, microphone input may be used to detect a clap trigger. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in your browser settings.
  • Room image: An image you capture or select may be used to create the background effect.
  • Extension settings: Preferences and configuration may be stored to preserve your choices.
  • Website access: The extension may interact with supported pages, such as Google Meet, to provide its advertised functionality.

3. How information is used

Information accessed by PhantomCam is used to operate the features you choose, such as applying camera effects, using your selected room image, detecting a clap trigger, and remembering your preferences.

The precise handling of camera frames, microphone audio, and images depends on the extension's implementation. This policy should be kept aligned with the actual code and any network requests made by the extension.

4. Sharing and storage

Extension settings or images may be stored locally if the extension's code uses browser storage. Any information sent to remote servers, analytics providers, or third-party services must be disclosed here, including what is sent and why.

Before publishing, verify the extension's network requests and storage behavior. Do not claim that information is never transmitted unless you have confirmed that this is true.

5. Retention and deletion

Information saved by the extension may remain until you delete it, clear the relevant browser data, uninstall the extension, or use a deletion feature provided by the extension. The exact behavior depends on the extension's implementation.

You can manage camera and microphone permissions through your browser settings.

6. Third-party services

PhantomCam may be used alongside services such as Google Meet. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. PhantomCam is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google unless explicitly stated by the developer.

Any analytics, remote processing, or other third-party services used by PhantomCam should be identified in this policy.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PhantomCam's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
